# Pillar 1: Commercial AI Workflows

## Executive Sponsor

Chief Digital Officer (CDO)

## Program Owner

Head of Commercial AI

## Duration

12 Months

## Purpose

Transform commercial operations by embedding AI into pricing, marketing, content creation, search visibility, and day-to-day ways of working.

The objective is not to deploy AI tools. The objective is to create measurable business value through AI-enabled commercial workflows.
